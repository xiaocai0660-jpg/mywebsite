About BR Jalan Securities

BR Jalan Securities Pvt Ltd is a SEBI-registered intermediary providing broking and depository services to Indian investors. Since inception, our focus has been to combine market experience with disciplined processes and transparent communication.

Our Mission

To help investors protect and grow their wealth through research-backed ideas, risk-managed execution and long-term relationships built on trust.

Our Approach

  • Research driven: Ideas start from data and fundamentals, not rumours.
  • Process oriented: Clear rules for entries, exits and position sizing.
  • Risk aware: Focus on capital preservation before return maximisation.
  • Compliance focused: Operations aligned with SEBI and exchange norms.
