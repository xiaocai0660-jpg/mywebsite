Investor Portal

A single login where clients can view statements portfolio snapshots and plan progress in a secure environment provided by the exchanges and depositories

How to access your account

For live trading account details contract notes and daily statements clients are redirected to the authorised back-office and exchange platforms through secure links

  • Existing clients receive login credentials directly from our operations team at the time of account activation
  • Passwords can be reset through the respective back-office or depository portals using registered mobile and email
  • Statements can be downloaded for contract notes margin positions ledger and holdings

For any difficulty in logging in or reconciling your statements you may contact our support desk using the details given on the contact page

Portal features

  • Portfolio snapshot – live view of your holdings and valuation as provided by the back-office system
  • Reports download – contract notes ledger margin reports and tax statements
  • Plan tracking – view capital allocation to the 10th Plan and other strategies where applicable
  • Service requests – submit update requests for bank details nomination and other KYC linked information

Note This information section is for explanation only The actual login takes place on the exchange or back-office portals as per SEBI and exchange regulations
