Three Core Investment Strategies

We rely on logic, systems, and execution mechanisms to plan every opportunity, rather than speculating on concepts or betting on ups and downs.

1. AI-Smart Short-Term

Logic: Uses neural network models to predict trends and backtest data, combining price fluctuations, capital flows, and news sentiment.

Automation: Full-process automation; no need to watch the market.

Returns: High Net Worth Funds (> 500,000₹) expected returns of more than 10% daily.

2. Block-Trade Strategy

Logic: Strategic high-discount arbitrage trading. We negotiate with shareholders to buy at a discount (e.g., ₹90) and sell at market price (e.g., ₹100).

Advantage: Profit can be locked even if the stock price remains the same the next day.

3. IPO - Offline Placement

Logic: We participate in the "offline placement channel" to obtain Primitive IPO Shares in advance at a low price.

Growth: The profit of each round is typically 80%-200%. This is the most explosive sector.
