10th Collective Investment Scheme

A hybrid strategy combining AI rotation, bulk deals, IPO allocations, and private equity access

Plan Overview

The 10th Collective Investment Scheme is designed for investors who want a structured, multi-layer growth strategy rather than one-time trades. Capital is deployed across AI-based intraday rotations, institutional bulk-deal entries, offline IPO allocations, and selected private equity opportunities.

Objective

To create accelerated capital growth over a 4–6 month window while keeping strict risk-control rules and liquidity.

Time Horizon

Recommended holding period: 4 to 6 months, with interim exits possible as per cycle completion.

Liquidity

Exit requests normally processed within 24 hours via regulated Nodal Account.

Investment Tiers & Minimum Amount

Tier Minimum Investment Strategy Focus Indicative Target
Tier 1 10,000 AI intraday rotation, single-cycle trades, weekly profit booking Target 35%+ per month in favourable conditions
Tier 2 1,00,000 AI rotation + bulk-deal participation with discounts 3–5× potential over ~4 months on successful cycles
Tier 3 5,00,000 and above Full-stack: AI rotation, deep-discount bulk deals, offline IPOs, private equity Shares + bonus + potential dividends from equity allocations

Targets are indicative and not guaranteed. All returns depend on market conditions and execution.

Risk & Safeguards

  • Funds handled via SEBI-authorised, RBI-regulated Nodal Account
  • Client money not mixed with company operational funds
  • Strict position sizing and stop-loss discipline in AI rotations
  • Bulk deals only with transparent discount and clear exit logic
  • Regular reporting and performance review

Who Should Join

  • Investors who can commit at least one cycle (4–6 months)
  • HNIs looking for higher alpha compared to standard mutual funds
  • Professionals with idle capital looking for active yet guided strategies
  • Clients who value transparency and risk-managed execution
