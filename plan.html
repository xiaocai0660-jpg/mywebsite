The 10th Collective Investment Scheme

prepared investors

A structured scheme that combines AI-assisted intraday rotation, negotiated bulk deals and selective IPO / pre-IPO opportunities under one coordinated execution framework.

Plan objective

The 10th Collective Investment Scheme is designed for investors who already understand equity markets and are looking for a more structured way to participate in short-term and primary market opportunities with clear rules.

Capital is rotated between intraday AI signals, bulk deals at negotiated discounts and selective primary / pre-IPO allocations, based on the market environment and risk discipline of our research desk.

Key highlights

  • Professional research desk driven by data and policy.
  • Clear ticket-size slabs and entry requirements.
  • Single point of coordination for all executions.
  • Focus on risk control and capital protection first.

Investment tiers & strategy mix

Tier 1 — AI Rotation

Entry from 10,000

  • Intraday AI rotation list for short-term compounding.
  • Pre-defined stop loss and booking rules.
  • Suited for investors starting with smaller capital.

Tier 2 — Bulk Deal Window

Preferred from 1,00,000

  • Institutional-grade bulk deals at negotiated discounts.
  • Limited slots, allocation on first-come prepared basis.
  • Used to average into strong names with price advantage.

Tier 3 — IPO & Pre-IPO

From 5,00,000 and above

  • Offline IPO allocations and selective pre-IPO ideas.
  • Priority given to clients with existing track record.
  • Structured exit view and periodic review with our desk.

How the scheme works

1

Profile & suitability check

Our team understands your capital, risk profile and time horizon before suggesting the appropriate tier and mix inside the scheme.

2

Onboarding & documentation

KYC, trading and demat accounts are mapped to the scheme and standard operating rules are explained to you in detail.

3

Execution & communication

Trades, bulk deals and allocations are executed through exchange systems, with regular communication on entries, exits and utilisation.

4

Review & re-balancing

The desk reviews your deployment and may re-balance between AI rotation, bulk deals and primary issues depending on markets.

Risk considerations

  • All investments in equities and equity-related products carry market risk.
  • No return is guaranteed; past performance does not indicate future performance.
  • Short-term trading involves the possibility of rapid drawdowns.
  • Allocations in IPO / pre-IPO opportunities are subject to availability and regulatory norms.

Important

The 10th Collective Investment Scheme is intended only for investors who understand equity risk and are comfortable with disciplined execution rules. Please speak to our team before deciding your participation level.
