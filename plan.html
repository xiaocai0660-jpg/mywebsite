The 10th collective investment scheme

AI rotation combined with bulk-deal opportunities

A time-bound collective plan combining intraday AI rotation block-deal allocations and selected IPO / pre-IPO ideas for prepared investors

Plan objective

The 10th Plan is designed for clients who are willing to allocate capital for a focused period and follow clear rules Our desk aims to compound short-term profits from AI rotation and deploy a part of capital into discounted block deals or primary market opportunities when available

  • Time frame typically 4–6 months per cycle
  • Live intraday rotation signals with same-day square-off
  • Allocation into block / bulk deals when risk-reward is favourable
  • Strict risk and exposure limits pre-defined
Key terms

Entry requirement

Minimum suggested capital band for meaningful participation

  • Suggested working range 3,00,000 – 5,00,000
  • Capital remains in client’s own trading account under full visibility
  • No lock-in on funds client can withdraw unused balance anytime as per broker rules
How it works

Execution framework

Step 1

KYC & risk profile

Client completes KYC and shares basic risk profile Our desk discusses capital band expectations and comfort level

Step 2

Account mapping

Trading account is mapped to the 10th Plan strategy process Entries exits and risk limits are explained in simple language

Step 3

Daily execution

AI rotation signals are fired during market hours and block-deal / primary opportunities are executed when available Client receives regular updates

Step 4

Review & exit

At the end of the plan window profits and deployed positions are reviewed Client may choose to roll over into the next plan or book out
