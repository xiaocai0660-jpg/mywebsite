The 10th Collective Investment Plan

This plan is designed as a structured, rule-based framework for investors who wish to participate in a combination of AI-assisted stock rotation and selected opportunities, while keeping clear control on risk and exit discipline.

This is an illustration of the framework. It is not a guaranteed-return product and is always subject to market risks.

STRUCTURE

How the Plan Is Structured

  • Portfolio built using AI-assisted stock rotation lists plus human research review.
  • Short-cycle holding periods with pre-defined exit rules.
  • Optional participation in selected block / bulk opportunities for eligible investors.
  • Continuous monitoring during the active plan period.
ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility & Indicative Minimum Capital

  • Investors comfortable with equity-market risk.
  • Willingness to follow a rules-based approach for entries and exits.
  • Indicative minimum participation capital to be decided case-by-case based on investor profile and opportunity size.
PROCESS

Execution & Risk Management

  • Clear communication of suggested allocation, entry zone and protective stop levels.
  • Emphasis on controlling position size and avoiding over-concentration in single stocks.
  • Regular updates during the life of the plan on key events and exit signals.
  • Full transparency on charges as per agreed schedule.
IMPORTANT

Important Disclosures

  • The plan does not offer any guarantee or assured return.
  • All investments are subject to market risk, including possible loss of capital.
  • Investors should read the disclosure documents, risk factors and fee structure before participating.
  • Suitability assessment is essential before any execution.
