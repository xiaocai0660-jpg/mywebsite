How the plan is executed

  • All trades are routed through B R Jalan dealing desk
  • Each client is mapped to a risk profile and capital slab before execution
  • AI models provide rotation lists while final calls are taken by the research and dealing team
  • Exits are pre-defined through price bands or time-based rules and are communicated in advance wherever possible
  • Performance is monitored cycle by cycle instead of random single trades

Key risk disclosure

All investments in securities markets are subject to market risks Past performance is not a guarantee of future returns and there can be periods of drawdown despite disciplined execution

Clients are advised to allocate only that portion of capital which can be exposed to equity market volatility after considering their broader financial commitments
